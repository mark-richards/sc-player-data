## ✅ VALIDATED FINDINGS

### 1. DURABILITY IS A CONSISTENT PREDICTOR ✅

**Finding:** Players who played more games consistently outperformed those who played fewer games.

| Year | High Games Avg | Low Games Avg | Difference | Validated? |
|------|---------------|---------------|------------|------------|
| 2022 | 93.0 pts | 71.0 pts | **22.0 pts** | ✅ YES |
| 2023 | 94.1 pts | 76.3 pts | **17.8 pts** | ✅ YES |
| 2024 | 93.9 pts | 79.2 pts | **14.7 pts** | ✅ YES |
| 2025* | 94.4 pts | 79.0 pts | **15.4 pts** | ✅ YES |

**Average difference: 18.2 points across 2022-2024**

**Correlation: 0.339 (2025 data)**

**CONCLUSION:** Durability (games played in previous year) is a **STRONG and CONSISTENT** predictor of fantasy success. Players who played 20+ games averaged 15-22 points higher than injury-prone players.

**ACTION FOR 2026:**
- Heavily weight "2025 games played" in your rankings
- Downgrade players who missed 5+ games in 2025
- When comparing two similar players, ALWAYS take the more durable one

### 2. LATE-ROUND VALUE EXISTS ✅

**Finding:** Significant value can be found in late rounds (picks 50+), with elite players drafted outside top 50.

| Year | Late-Round Gems | Elite Avg (1-20) | Value Avg (101+) | Difference |
|------|----------------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| 2022 | **17 players** | 106.4 pts | 76.2 pts | -30.2 pts |
| 2023 | **14 players** | 108.2 pts | 77.2 pts | -31.0 pts |
| 2024 | **14 players** | 107.4 pts | 79.2 pts | -28.2 pts |

**Average: 15 late-round gems per year**

**Late-round gem definition:** Drafted after pick 50, but finished in top 50 by average points

**Notable Late-Round Gems:**
- **2022:** Rory Laird (pick 79, rank #2 overall!), Jack Sinclair (pick 91, rank #8)
- **2023:** Caleb Serong (pick 69, rank #17), Luke Ryan (pick 68, rank #20)
- **2024:** Adam Treloar (pick 67, rank #9), Dayne Zorko (pick 103, rank #18)

**CONCLUSION:** While elite picks DO perform better on average, there are **14-17 players EVERY YEAR** who get drafted late but finish in the top 50. This represents ~30% of top-50 finishers!

**ACTION FOR 2026:**
- Don't overpay to move up in draft order
- Hunt for undervalued players in rounds 5-15
- Use data-driven metrics (TPOR, durability, position scarcity) to identify these gems
- **Examples of 2026 candidates:** Look for players with 20+ games in 2025, strong TPOR, but ranked 50-100 in projections

## 📊 DRAFT TIER PERFORMANCE COMPARISON

**2022-2024 Average Performance by Draft Tier:**

| Tier | Avg Points | Avg Games | Avg TPOR |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Elite (1-20) | **107.3** | 20.4 | **839.7** |
| Premium (21-50) | **98.6** | 19.8 | **654.4** |
| Mid (51-100) | **88.0** | 18.5 | **453.8** |
| Value (101+) | **77.5** | 17.8 | **258.8** |

**Key Insights:**
1. Elite picks DO perform better on average (~30 pts higher than Value picks)
2. BUT the standard deviation is HIGH (10-17 pts) meaning lots of variance
3. ~15 players drafted 50+ finish in top 50 EVERY YEAR
4. Games played declines in later rounds (injury-prone players fall in draft)

**Interpretation:** Draft position matters, but NOT as much as people think. The "Value" tier underperforms by 30 points, but if you can identify the 15 gems in this tier using data, you can extract elite performance from late picks.
